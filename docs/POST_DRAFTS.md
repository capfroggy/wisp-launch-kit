**Title:** I wrote a practical playbook for launching a small wireless ISP with no investors — sharing it in case it's useful

**Body:**

I've spent time running a small wireless ISP in a tourist town — a genuinely annoying market to price because demand is a mix of long-term residents, local businesses, and tourists who won't sign a 12-month contract.

I wrote up everything I learned into a playbook: equipment choices that don't lock you into one vendor, how to actually price a mixed residential/business/tourist customer base (day passes matter more than you'd think), a service agreement template, an install checklist, and a pricing/breakeven calculator you can run in your browser.

Put it up here if anyone's in the same boat: [LINK]

Happy to answer questions about the tourist/day-pass pricing model in the comments too, since that part doesn't get covered much anywhere else.
